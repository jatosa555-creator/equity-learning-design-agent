# High-Impact Practices (HIP): choose context-fit pedagogies

The project `Formative Assessment -3D` workshop material includes teaching practices (Thai source vocabulary rendered as condensed English):

1. **Design learning for intended outcomes** — explicit, high-quality task linked to the objective.
2. **Cognitively appropriate/challenging tasks** — scaffold cognitive demand without lowering expectations unfairly.
3. **Purposeful partner/group work** — accountability, equitable role/voice rotation, collaboration with clear learning purpose.
4. **Formative assessment** — observe ideas/misconceptions during the task and adapt instruction; feedback to improve.
5. **Learner-centered dialogue** — opportunities for reasoning, explaining, challenging and listening.
6. **Routine writing** — quick written reasoning, notes or reflection accessible in multiple modalities.
7. **Inquiry & project-based learning** — investigate, test ideas and apply concepts authentically.
8. **Learning goal setting** — short visible objectives and meaningful reflection on next steps.

**This is a teaching menu, not 8 things every lesson must contain.** For MVP choose 1–2 aligned interventions per plan and give concrete lesson moves, e.g., Think–Pair–Share + structured role rotation, low-stakes exit ticket, CPA (Concrete–Pictorial–Abstract) for appropriate math topics, guided inquiry/5E where apt.
