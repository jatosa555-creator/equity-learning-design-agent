# Six-point Equity & Gender Equality Compass (design-support tool)

An additional 6-point **Classroom-Level Equity & Gender-Equality Compass** in Act 4, distinct from the rubric's list/assessment anchors:

1. **Equitable Access** — how learners with differing preparation, abilities, language, resource constraints and identities enter and take part.
2. **Role & Voice** — substantive roles, not symbolic names, with opportunity for each learner to contribute.
3. **Reality-Based Data Use** — use qualitative/quantitative classroom signals to spot patterns and make fair adjustments.
4. **Agency & Co-Decision Making** — learners affect decisions about activities and ways of demonstrating learning.
5. **Real-Life Relevance** — inclusive local examples, life experiences and varied role models/career routes.
6. **Discussion & Reflection** — meaningful, facilitated participation, multiple modes of reflection, actual response to learner feedback.

Use for **design improvements**, not duplicate scoring against the other six dimensions. In audits, state which source lens you use.
