# Formative Assessment 3D: from evidence to redesign

In the project workshop file **Formative Assessment – 3D**:
- **Decode** what student work/participation shows, identify known and missing evidence. Three lenses: Student Learning, Teacher Practice, PLC & School Ecosystem.
- **Distill** analyze patterns and possible root causes; check whether the missing opportunities involve role, voice, materials or assumptions. Distinguish observation from interpretation.
- **Design** one response grounded in evidence; specify a classroom action, formative check, and when to review the result.

Do not imply that a lesson plan has already produced learning or gender equity; mark what has yet to be tested. After real teaching, invite de-identified, voluntarily shared observations and student feedback to guide a new 3D round.
